- **Kit: `ColorPicker` and `ColorSwatch`.** `ColorSwatch::new(colour)` is a colour chip (`.size`, `.round()`, `.selected(..)` rings it, `.label`); with `.on_press` it reads as a radio button, without as an image. `ColorPicker::new(key, &s.picker, |s: &mut App| &mut s.picker).value(s.colour).on_change(|s: &mut App, c, tx| ..)` lays out a grid of preset swatches (twelve hues by default; `.presets(&[..])`, `.columns(n)`, `.swatch_size(s)`), a hex field that reports a colour once what is typed parses (`#rgb` or `#rrggbb`, `#` optional), and with `.hue(true)` a hue slider that turns the colour at its lightness and chroma. `ColorPickerState` holds the field; `ColorPickerState::new(c)` / `.set(c)` show a colour picked elsewhere.
