- **Kit: state badges, coloured buttons, wider listboxes and popovers.** `BadgeVariant` gains `Success`, `Warning`, `Danger` and `Info`, painted from the kit's `StateColors` (soft fill, text colour, border); a `match` over `BadgeVariant` needs the new arms. `IconButtonVariant` gains `Danger` and `Success` (the neutral button with the role's icon colour), and `Button::tint(fill, ink)` / `IconButton::tint(fill, ink)` paint a button in an app's own colours, with hover and press laying the ink over the fill. A `Select`'s listbox is now at least as wide as a field trigger (`SelectAppearance::Field`, `Select::width`) instead of a fixed 160 floor, and `Select::menu_width(w)` sets it exactly. `Popover::max_width` / `min_width` lift the 320 / 200 bounds; `HoverCard` takes the same two.
