- **Kit: `Avatar`, `Skeleton`, and a styleable `Progress`.** `Avatar::new(name)` shows the name's initials on a colour picked from it (the same every build) and, with `.image(&pixels)`, the picture over them once it has pixels; `.size`, `.shape(AvatarShape::Round | Rounded)`, `.status(colour)` for a presence dot, and `.fill` / `.ink` / `.initials` overrides. It reads as an image named for the person. `Skeleton::new()` (or `Skeleton::circle(d)`) is a loading placeholder that pulses between full and half opacity and holds full under reduced motion; it stays out of the accessibility tree unless given a `.label`. `Progress::indeterminate()` names the sweeping bar `Progress::new(None)` already drew, and `.height(h)`, `.color(c)` and `.track(c)` restyle the bar (a bar 4 points or thinner drops the track's border).
