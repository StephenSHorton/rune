- **Rune runs on Android (prototype).** An app's `android_main` hands Rune the activity with
  `rune::android::init(app)` and then calls `rune::run` as on the desktop; winit's
  NativeActivity backend comes with the Android target, so the app needs no winit dependency of
  its own. When Android takes the native window away (the app goes to the background) a window
  drops its surface, and when it comes back the window gets a new surface on the GPU it kept
  and draws its state as it was, instead of staying black. `examples/android-hello` and
  `scripts/android-apk.sh` build a signed APK with cargo and the SDK's build tools, without
  Gradle or Java. Not there yet: on-screen keyboard text input, safe-area insets, the back
  button, clipboard, opening links and accessibility.
