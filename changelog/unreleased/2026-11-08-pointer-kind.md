- **Presses say what drove them.** `PointerKind` (`Mouse` or `Touch`): `Tx::pointer_kind()` in `on_press` and `on_drag`, `Drag::pointer_kind()`, and a new `PointerEvent::kind` field for `on_pointer_move_local` and `on_secondary_press`, so an app can open on a tap what a mouse opens on hover. A finger is `Touch` wherever the host sends touches through `Runtime::touch` (winit touchscreens on Windows, Linux, iOS and Android, and the web); a pen is whatever the platform sends it as. `PointerEvent` is `#[non_exhaustive]`, so the new field breaks no app.
