- **Popups hang from a text field's caret.** `El::anchored_to_caret(key, side, align, gap)` (or `.anchored_at_caret()` after `anchored` / `anchored_fit`) places an overlay beside the caret line of the focused field keyed `key` (or inside it), flipping and staying in the window as `El::anchored` does and following typing, scrolling and IME composition in the frame the caret moves; without focus it sits beside the field. Kit's `PopupList::at_caret` / `anchored_to_caret` and `Popover::at_caret` use it, `PopupList::header` adds a muted heading line above the rows, and `--tree` lists a focused field's `caret`. `Anchor` gains a `caret` field.
