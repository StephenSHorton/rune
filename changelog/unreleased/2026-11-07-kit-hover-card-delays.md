- **Kit: `HoverCard` opens and closes itself on hover.** `HoverCard::hover(&s.card, |s: &mut App| &mut s.card)` keeps a `HoverCardState` in the app's state: the card opens once the pointer rests on the trigger (700 ms) and stays open while the pointer is over the trigger or the card, closing 300 ms after it leaves both, so the pointer can cross the gap into the card. `HoverCard::delays(open, close)` tunes both; `HoverCardState::close` shuts it at once. `.open(bool)` still works for app-timed cards.
